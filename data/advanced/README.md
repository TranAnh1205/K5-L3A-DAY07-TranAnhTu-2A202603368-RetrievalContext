# Separate 100-page bonus source

**Book:** OpenStax, Rice University, *Principles of Management* (2019 edition), [official book page](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-management). The [OpenStax preface](https://openstax.org/books/principles-management/pages/preface) describes the current online book's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms; this study packet is used noncommercially with attribution and the source PDF retains an OpenStax attribution on every page. Individual third-party figures may have their own credits in their captions. The packet was obtained from [Nguyễn Đắc Thể's public Day 7 fork](https://github.com/NguyenDacThe/K5-L3A-DAY07-NguyenDacThe-2A202603529-RetrievalContext/blob/main/data/advanced/source_100pages.pdf), which extracted 100 consecutive physical pages (original book PDF pages 89–188). It is used here as a **source document**, not as a benchmark or result. No question, gold label, code, trace or score from that fork is reused.

`source_100pages.pdf`: exactly 100 packet pages, SHA-256 `f44a7503c5c14d54108aef0ed35f1db3c4b57c327b710da5d44d9216ffcead6c`. Packet page `n` maps to original physical PDF page `n+88`. The full original book is longer; this submission indexes the **100-page study packet**, not the whole 605-page book. The packet contains text, tables, vector diagrams, raster images and charts. It is included so a reviewer can verify all evidence without another download.

Run `python -m src.bonus_index` to regenerate `page_index.json` from the PDF. It extracts all text on all 100 pages and records page IDs, source, original-page mapping, caption labels, text/image/drawing inventory, caption bounding boxes and a full-page image locator. At answer time, `src.bonus_agent` searches the page index and renders selected **original packet pages** to the vision model. Thus chart/table/diagram details are read from the PDF pixels rather than treating an autogenerated caption as ground truth. The index has 100/100 pages with text, 76 Exhibit/Table labels, 33 pages with raster images, and 77 pages with vector drawings. Text extraction may scramble table order, so the page image remains authoritative. All pages are searchable and renderable; no page was intentionally skipped.

`benchmark.json` was fixed before the bonus dev/test runs. Its SHA-256 is `2e8efa369fb6a79b6d27e049ffc162532adff0fe7db49f763f1ae329118a0853`; `benchmark_dev.json` and `benchmark_test.json` are split views. Evidence records give packet page, original page, exact Exhibit/Table or section locator, a figure/table region in PDF coordinates when applicable, and the row/column or visible relation to check. For text questions, `detail` gives a checkable excerpt. The agent receives only question text. The three no-evidence dev and six no-evidence test cases include document-wide absence reasons; page images and indexed text should be checked again during manual grading.
